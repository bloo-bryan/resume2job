Data Analyst

Location: Portland, OR (Remote-friendly)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Company
Metric Health is a healthcare analytics startup helping hospital systems reduce readmission rates through data-driven insights. Our small but growing team works directly with clinical stakeholders to turn raw data into actionable dashboards and reports.

Requirements
- 1+ years of experience in a data analyst or business intelligence role
- Strong SQL skills for querying and transforming data across multiple tables and databases
- Advanced Excel proficiency including pivot tables, VLOOKUP, and data modeling
- Experience with data visualization tools and best practices for presenting insights to stakeholders
- Attention to detail and ability to validate data quality
- Strong communication skills for translating analytical findings into business recommendations

Nice to Have
- Programming experience in Python for data manipulation and automation
- Experience with Tableau for building interactive dashboards
- Familiarity with Power BI for reporting and self-service analytics
- Knowledge of basic statistical methods
- Experience working with healthcare or other regulated data

Education
- B.A. or B.S. in Mathematics, Economics, Business, Statistics, or a related field required

Compensation: $65,000 - $90,000 base + bonus
